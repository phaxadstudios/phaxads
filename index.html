01 Positioning

A research company
that also uses its own technology.

Phaxad Technologies Limited is an independent AI, quantitative research and proprietary software company. It develops intelligent analytical systems, models, algorithms and technology infrastructure, with algorithmic trading research as the first area of focus.

The company operates on two connected revenue streams. It uses its own technology to generate returns — trading with its own capital under its own systems — and it licenses approved technology to companies that meet Phaxad's review requirements.

The bot is one of several technologies. Phaxad's broader technology stack — analytical models, signal engines, event readers, risk frameworks and infrastructure — can also be licensed individually or in combination. Phaxad reviews each request carefully and only grants a licence when it is satisfied the arrangement is appropriate, responsible and fully compliant.

"We use our own technology, and we licence it to others — but only after review."

02 Revenue model

Two revenue streams.
One technology stack.

Phaxad generates revenue through its own use of its technology, and through licensed access to approved technology. Both operate alongside each other — this is not an either/or model.

Stream 01
Own use

Phaxad uses its technology

Phaxad uses its own analytical systems, models and infrastructure to generate returns from its own capital. The company's technology is designed to work in real conditions before being offered to anyone else.

  • Uses its own systems on its own capital
  • Generates returns from that use
  • Does not manage third-party money
  • Subject to applicable regulatory requirements
Stream 02
Licensed access

Phaxad licenses approved technology

Approved companies can license specific technologies from Phaxad's stack — models, signal engines, analytics or infrastructure — under formal agreements. Phaxad reviews every request before granting.

  • Licensing fees for approved access
  • Subscription options for ongoing use
  • Profit-share arrangements where permissible
  • Review required before any licence
What can be licensed

Not just the trading bot.

Phaxad's technology stack spans multiple layers. Licensees can request access to specific components — individually or in combination — depending on their needs and after Phaxad's review.

Analytical models

Machine-learning models for pattern recognition, regime detection and market structure analysis.

Signal engines

The scoring and decision layer that combines model outputs into actionable intelligence.

Risk frameworks

The risk guard layer — position sizing, exposure limits, circuit-breakers and override systems.

Event readers

Language models that classify news and economic releases into structured signals.

Infrastructure

Data pipelines, feature layers and analytical infrastructure built to support the full stack.

The trading bot

The full system — models, signals and risk — available under licence for approved use.

Not a bot sale

Phaxad does not sell its trading bot or any of its technology outright. All access is through licence, subscription or profit-share terms.

Not licence-on-request

Phaxad reviews every licence request and only grants a licence when it is satisfied. Being interested is not enough — approval is required.

Not managed accounts

Phaxad does not manage money on behalf of third parties. It provides licensed technology — not a fund, not a managed service.

03 Licensing & review

Review first.
Licence after.

Phaxad reviews every licence request carefully. A licence is only granted when Phaxad is fully satisfied that the arrangement is appropriate, responsible, and compliant. Being interested in the technology does not guarantee access.

Review process

How a licence request is assessed

fig. 01 · process

    No licence is granted by default. Phaxad reserves the right to decline any request. Approval depends on compliance with applicable law, the licensee's regulatory standing, the nature of intended use, and Phaxad's own assessment that the arrangement is responsible.

    Phaxad must be satisfied that
    • The licensee holds or will hold the necessary regulatory permissions for its intended use.
    • The technology will be used within its designed purpose — not repurposed, resold or misrepresented.
    • The commercial terms are clear, fair and legally sound — including fees, subscription and any profit-share element.
    • The arrangement protects Phaxad's IP, users and reputation.
    • Any applicable securities, digital-asset or financial-services rules are respected.

        07 Regulatory position

        Regulated activity
        stays regulated.

        Financial markets, digital assets and licensing arrangements are regulated areas. Phaxad is not currently authorised to trade, to operate a fund, to offer financial services, or to enter into revenue-sharing arrangements in any jurisdiction.

        Any live operations in Nigeria — including Phaxad's own use of its technology, proposed crypto-trading, licensing, subscription and profit-sharing arrangements — will require appropriate legal and regulatory review under applicable securities and digital-asset rules before starting.

        Not currently authorised to trade

        Phaxad has no trading authorisation and does not currently trade on its own account. Any such activity would require appropriate approvals first.

        Regulatory review before live operations

        Proposed crypto-trading, licensing, subscription and profit-sharing arrangements will be reviewed under applicable securities and digital-asset rules in Nigeria before any live operations begin.

        No investment advice

        Phaxad builds software. It does not provide investment, financial or trading advice and does not act as a broker or adviser.

        Future regulated fields

        Aerospace, defence and similar activities would only be pursued with the necessary approvals and in line with applicable law.
